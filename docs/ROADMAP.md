# AIOS Roadmap

**Version:** 0.0.1

Development advances by earned autonomy. Each phase must produce evidence that its controls work before the next phase receives broader tools, resources, or external impact.

## Phase 0 — Constitutional foundation

Define organizational sovereignty, human-reserved powers, core ontology, authority levels, delegation, institutional memory, and audit requirements. Establish versioning and amendment procedures. Produce threat, conflict, and consistency reviews of the constitutional documents.

**Exit evidence:** the normative documents are approved by the human owner; core terms and precedence are consistent; representative scenarios identify a permitted action, required approval, refusal, escalation, and emergency suspension.

## Phase 1 — Minimal kernel

Implement organization and actor identities, an append-only event log, typed events, goal and task state machines, authority evaluation, approval requests, resource accounting, institutional records, and audit queries. Separate actor identity from model execution. Provide deterministic policy tests, replay, idempotency, credential isolation, and human kill switches.

**Exit evidence:** a complete proposal-to-outcome trace can be reconstructed; unauthorized and expired actions fail closed; event replay restores state; budgets stop overruns; suspension prevents further execution; model replacement preserves organizational continuity.

## Phase 2 — First receptionist/router employee

Create a least-privileged AI employee that receives inbound events, classifies intent and sensitivity, retrieves authorized context, creates or links tasks, and routes work to a human or eligible role. It may acknowledge receipt and draft responses but cannot make commitments, spend funds, disclose sensitive data, or invent authority.

**Exit evidence:** evaluated routing accuracy and escalation recall meet approved thresholds; prompt injection and data-boundary tests pass; every route is attributable; uncertain or high-risk requests reliably reach human review.

## Phase 3 — Research organization

Add bounded researcher, analyst, critic, and synthesizer roles. Support source capture, claim/evidence graphs, provenance, confidence and validity tracking, contradiction handling, research budgets, and reproducible reports. Temporary specialist workers may operate only under the delegation policy.

**Exit evidence:** research briefs distinguish evidence from inference, cite retrievable sources, expose material conflicts, remain reproducible after model replacement, and comply with privacy, licensing, and resource limits.

## Phase 4 — Supervised business discovery

Use the research organization to identify and compare lawful business opportunities. Define human-approved discovery goals and constraints; evaluate customer need, competition, feasibility, economics, legal exposure, safety, and ethical risk. Conduct interviews, simulations, and reversible experiments without binding commitments or misleading participants.

**Exit evidence:** a human reviews an auditable opportunity dossier and explicitly approves, rejects, or requests further validation; experiments remain within consent, spending, brand, and contact limits; no launch authority is implied by discovery approval.

## Phase 5 — Controlled execution

Introduce narrowly scoped execution roles and tools for an approved business: sandboxed asset creation, supervised communications, capped procurement, monitored service operation, customer support routing, accounting records, and incident response. Expand from drafts to reversible pilots, then to consequential actions only through explicit human approvals and demonstrated controls.

**Exit evidence:** the business can complete a bounded operating cycle with reconciled resources, measurable outcomes, privacy and security controls, human approval of reserved decisions, tested rollback and suspension, and a complete audit trail.

## Cross-cutting gates

Every phase maintains constitutional conformance, least privilege, evidence-based evaluation, retention and deletion controls, security testing, incident review, cost measurement, and accessible human oversight. Advancement is reversible. A failed gate reduces authority or returns the system to an earlier phase; roadmap progress never overrides the Constitution.
